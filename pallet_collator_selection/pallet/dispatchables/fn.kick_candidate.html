pub fn kick_candidate<T: Config>(who: T::AccountId)

Forcibly remove a candidate from the active set.

This will immediately remove the candidate from the candidates list and unbond their deposit after slashing it accordigly to SlashRatio.

This call will fail if removing the candidate would bring the total number of candidates below the minimum threshold.
